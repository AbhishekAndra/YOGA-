Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any prior yoga experience?
Not at all! Hatha Yoga, Yin & Restore, and Meditation are all designed for complete beginners. Your instructor will always offer simpler variations of every posture so you can practice safely and comfortably from day one.
What should I bring to my first class?
Just yourself and comfortable clothing. We provide mats, blocks, bolsters, straps, and blankets for all students. If you have your own mat you love, bring it along — but it's absolutely not required.
How early should I arrive?
We recommend arriving 10–15 minutes before your first class. This gives you time to settle in, introduce yourself to the instructor, ask any questions, and find the perfect spot in the studio without any rush.
Can I switch classes if I book the wrong one?
Of course! You can reschedule any booking up to 4 hours before the class start time via your customer dashboard or by contacting us. We're always happy to help you find the right class for where you are today.
Is Yoga Sculpt suitable if I'm a beginner?
Yoga Sculpt is marked as Advanced as it combines flowing yoga sequences with light resistance training. We recommend attending at least 8–10 regular Hatha or Vinyasa classes first so you have a comfortable foundation in the movements before adding weights.
Do you offer private or one-on-one classes?
Yes! All Studio Member and Wellness Pro members receive private instructor sessions as part of their plan. Others can book standalone 1-on-1 sessions by contacting us. Private sessions are tailored entirely to your goals, pace, and physical needs.
