Hatha Yoga

Hatha is the timeless foundation of all yoga — a classical practice where breath meets precise alignment. We guide you through foundational postures with care and intention, building body awareness at a pace that feels right for you. Perfect for beginners or anyone returning to the mat. There's no rush here, just purposeful practice.

  • Build a strong, safe postural foundation
  • Develop breath awareness and body confidence
  • Find calm and clarity through intentional movement

  • Cultivate a moving meditation practice

Yin & Restore

An invitation to finally slow down. Yin & Restore uses long-held, floor-based postures to gently release deep connective tissue, quiet an overactive mind, and soothe an overstimulated nervous system. No effort required — just surrender. Find the stillness your body has been quietly asking for all along.

  • Release deep tension in joints and connective tissue
  • Calm the nervous system and reduce stress
  • Cultivate patience, surrender, and inner quiet

Meditation

Stillness is a practice, not a destination. Our guided sessions weave breathwork (pranayama), visualisation, and mindfulness into a dedicated space for inner quiet. Attend as a standalone ritual to anchor your week, or pair it with any movement class to deepen the experience. Your breath is always the anchor.

  • Reduce anxiety and mental chatter
  • Develop a sustainable daily mindfulness practice
  • Improve focus, sleep quality, and emotional resilience

Yoga Sculpt

Where intention meets intensity. Yoga Sculpt fuses dynamic yoga flows with light weights and high-rep functional movements to build a strong, resilient, capable body — from the inside out. Expect to be challenged and leave feeling genuinely powerful. This is yoga that works hard, and so will you.

  • Build lean muscle and functional strength
  • Increase metabolic capacity and endurance
  • Develop mind-muscle connection through intentional movement
