About the Studio

Empowering you
through ancient wisdom

We are a forward-thinking wellness studio rooted in authentic yoga tradition. Our experienced teachers guide every student — from first-timers to seasoned practitioners — through purposeful movement, conscious breathing, and mindful rest.

Founded on the belief that wellness is not a destination but a way of living, our studio offers a warm, inclusive space where transformation unfolds gently and sustainably.

Our Classes

Every body has
its own rhythm

Explore our full range of yoga styles and wellness offerings, each taught by certified instructors in a small-group setting.

☀️
Hatha Yoga

A classical approach focusing on alignment, breath, and foundational postures. Perfect for beginners and those who prefer a measured pace.

All Levels
🌊
Vinyasa Flow

Dynamic, breath-led sequences that build strength, flexibility, and a moving meditation. Each class is unique — guided by the teacher's intuition.

Intermediate
🌙
Yin & Restore

Slow, floor-based poses held for several minutes to release deep connective tissue and calm the nervous system. Pure, nourishing stillness.

All Levels

Meditation

Guided sits covering breathwork, visualisation, and mindfulness techniques. Available as standalone sessions or paired with yoga classes.

All Levels
